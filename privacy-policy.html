1. Introduction

Welcome to Social00. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle your information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

Social00 provides free online tools for content creation, image editing, academic assistance, and SEO optimization. By using our website and services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

We believe in minimal data collection and maximum transparency. Our tools are designed to work primarily in your browser, meaning most processing happens on your device without sending data to our servers.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit Social00, we automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing behavior:

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type, screen resolution
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns, referring websites
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser language, time zone, cookies and similar technologies
  • Performance Data: Page load times, errors encountered, feature usage statistics

2.2 Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when using our tools:

  • Tool Inputs: Text, images, or data you enter into our tools for processing
  • Saved Preferences: Settings and preferences stored in your browser's local storage
  • Feedback: Any comments, suggestions, or feedback you choose to submit

Important: Most of our tools process data entirely in your browser. Images, text, and other inputs typically never leave your device unless explicitly stated otherwise.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number) unless you voluntarily provide it
  • Payment information (we don't sell anything)
  • Precise geolocation data
  • Biometric data
  • Social media account information

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

3.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience. We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance functionality and analyze website performance.

3.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality, such as remembering your theme preference (dark/light mode)
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website through services like Google Analytics
  • Advertising Cookies: Used by Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements based on your interests
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and choices, such as recently used tools or saved calculations

3.3 Local Storage

We use browser local storage to save your preferences and recent tool usage locally on your device. This data never leaves your browser and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

3.4 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies (may affect website functionality)
  • Clear cookies when closing your browser

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

4.1 How We Use AdSense

Social00 uses Google AdSense to display advertisements that help support our free tools and services. Google AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your previous visits to our website and other websites on the internet.

4.2 Personalized Advertising

Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4.3 Third-Party Ad Networks

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website. These vendors may collect information about your online activities across different websites to provide relevant advertising.

4.4 Your Advertising Choices

You have several options to control advertising:

  • Opt out of personalized ads through Google Ads Settings
  • Use browser extensions that block ads
  • Enable "Do Not Track" in your browser settings
  • Visit the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page

5. Analytics Services

5.1 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit our site, what pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to coming to our site.

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect and analyze information about website usage. This information is used to compile reports and help us improve our services.

5.2 What Analytics Data We Collect

  • Page views and session duration
  • Traffic sources and referral information
  • Device and browser information
  • Geographic location (country/city level only)
  • User interactions with tools and features

5.3 Opting Out of Analytics

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6.1 External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites that are not operated by us. We have no control over the content and practices of these sites and cannot accept responsibility for their privacy policies.

6.2 Third-Party Tools and APIs

Some of our tools may integrate with third-party services. When you use these features, you may be subject to the privacy policies of those third-party services. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before use.

6.3 Social Media

Our website may include social media features, such as share buttons. These features may collect your IP address and set cookies to enable proper functionality. Your interactions with these features are governed by the privacy policies of the respective social media companies.

7. Data Security

7.1 How We Protect Your Data

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure hosting infrastructure
  • Regular security assessments and updates
  • Limited access to personal information
  • Client-side processing for sensitive operations

7.2 Data Retention

We retain automatically collected information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Analytics data is typically retained for 26 months. Data stored in your browser's local storage remains until you clear it.

7.3 Data Breaches

In the unlikely event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request information about the data we collect about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt-Out: Opt out of certain data collection and advertising practices
  • Data Portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format

8.2 Browser Controls

You can control data collection through your browser settings:

  • Clear cookies and local storage
  • Enable "Do Not Track" signals
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Use private/incognito browsing mode

8.3 California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to request deletion of your information.

8.4 European Privacy Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, and data portability.

9. Children's Privacy

Social00 is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information as quickly as possible.

For users between 13 and 18, we recommend obtaining parental consent before using our services.

Your continued use of Social00 after any changes to this Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
