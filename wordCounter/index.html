Free Online Word Counter Tool

Our word counter is a fast, accurate, and completely free tool designed for writers, students, bloggers, and content creators. Get instant statistics about your text including word count, character count, sentence count, paragraph count, and estimated reading time. Everything runs in your browser with no data sent to servers.

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box above
  2. Watch live updates as statistics appear in real-time
  3. Copy your text using the Copy button when done
  4. Clear the box to start fresh with new content

Your text is automatically saved in your browser, so you won't lose your work if you refresh the page.
