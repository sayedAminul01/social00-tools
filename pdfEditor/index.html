    What Is This PDF Editor?

    This tool lets you make real edits to a PDF — add text, drop in images or a hand-drawn signature, sketch freehand, cover content you want hidden from view, and rotate, delete or reorder pages — without installing anything or sending your document anywhere. It uses PDF.js to render pages for editing and pdf-lib to write the final file, both running entirely on your own device.

    Adding Text, Images & Signatures

    Pick the Text tool, click anywhere on the page, and type — you can choose the font, size and color before or after placing it. Images work the same way: upload a file or draw a signature on the built-in pad, and it's placed on the page as a box you can drag and resize. Text is limited to the three PDF Standard 14 fonts (Helvetica, Times Roman, Courier) in this version, since they need no extra font file to embed.

    Whiteout / Cover: What It Does (and Doesn't Do)

    The Whiteout tool draws an opaque box on top of content you want out of view — useful for cleaning up a scanned form or blocking out a stray mark. But it is a visual cover, not a deletion: the text or image underneath is still present in the PDF's data and can be recovered by removing the box or pulling content with another tool. Don't rely on it to hide anything genuinely sensitive.

    Managing Pages: Rotate, Delete & Reorder

    Every page gets a thumbnail in the sidebar with its own rotate, delete and move buttons — click a thumbnail to load that page into the main editor. Rotations, deletions and reordering are tracked as a plan and only applied to the file when you click Download, so the original document is never touched until you're ready.

    Why Your Edits Stay Real PDF Content

    Some tools flatten an edited page into a single image, which quietly destroys the original text layer — no more searching, selecting or copying text from that page, and everything looks blurrier when you zoom in. This editor draws your additions with pdf-lib's native text and vector commands instead, so the rest of the page keeps its original fonts, sharpness and searchability untouched.

    Privacy: Everything Happens in Your Browser

    • Rendering (PDF.js) and editing/export (pdf-lib) both run on your device

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

    No. This tool loads your PDF locally using PDF.js for rendering and pdf-lib for editing, both running entirely in your browser. Your file is never uploaded to any server — everything, including the final export, happens on your own device.

    Does the Whiteout tool actually redact or remove content from my PDF?

    No — and this is important. Whiteout only draws an opaque box on top of the existing content in the visual output. The original text or image underneath is still present in the PDF's data and could be recovered by removing the box or extracting content with other tools. Do not use Whiteout to hide sensitive data like SSNs or account numbers that must be permanently removed — genuine redaction requires stripping the underlying content, which this tool does not do.

    Why are added text and images real PDF content instead of a flattened image?

    Your edits are drawn using pdf-lib's native drawing commands, so the rest of the page keeps its original text layer, fonts, and image quality — nothing gets flattened or re-rendered as a picture. That means the untouched parts of your document stay fully searchable, selectable, and crisp at any zoom level.

    Can I use my own custom fonts for added text?

    Not in this version. Text is added using the three PDF Standard 14 fonts — Helvetica, Times Roman, and Courier — because they're built into every PDF viewer and need no extra font file to embed. Custom font upload may be added in a future version.

    What happens to pages I rotate or delete when I export?

    Rotations and deletions are only applied when you click Download — the original file on disk is never touched. Rotating a page queues a 90° turn that's applied to the exported copy, and deleting a page simply leaves it out of the new file; any reordering you've done in the sidebar is preserved too.

    Is this tool free?

    Yes — completely free, no account required, no watermark, and no page or file-size limit imposed by this tool. Everything runs locally in your browser.
