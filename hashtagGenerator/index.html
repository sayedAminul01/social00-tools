        How Many Hashtags Actually Work Best on Instagram

        Instagram technically allows up to 30 hashtags per post, but current best-practice guidance from most creators and marketers is that 3-8 highly relevant hashtags often outperform maxing out the limit. Treat 30 as a ceiling you can use, not a target you must hit — relevance beats volume.

        Why Mixing Hashtag Popularity Tiers Matters

        Using only mega-popular tags like #love or #instagood buries your post in a firehose of millions of posts within minutes. Using only ultra-niche tags limits how many people ever see it. A mix of mega, medium and niche tags gives you both short-term visibility and a realistic shot at ranking in a smaller tag's top posts.

        Caption or First Comment: Where to Place Hashtags

        Both placements work and are indexed the same way. Hashtags in the caption are simpler and visible immediately; hashtags in the first comment keep the caption itself cleaner and more readable. Pick one style for your account and stay consistent so your audience knows what to expect.

        Avoiding Banned or Shadowbanned Hashtags

        Platforms occasionally restrict individual hashtags that get flooded with spam or policy-violating content. If you're unsure about a tag you're about to use, search for it directly on the platform first and check that its hashtag page looks normal and active before adding it to your post.

        Frequently Asked Questions

        How many hashtags should I actually use on Instagram?

        Instagram allows up to 30, but current best-practice guidance is that 3-8 highly relevant hashtags often outperform maxing out the limit. Treat 30 as a ceiling, not a target.

        Do more hashtags always mean more reach?

        No. Stuffing a caption with 30 loosely-related tags can dilute relevance and look spammy. A smaller, genuinely relevant set tends to perform better than a maxed-out generic one.

        Should hashtags go in the caption or the first comment?

        Both work and are indexed the same way. Caption is simpler and immediate; first comment keeps the caption cleaner. Pick one style and stay consistent.

        How are these hashtags chosen — is this live trending data?

        No. This tool uses a curated bank of commonly-used hashtags per niche plus tags derived from what you type. The tier labels are general guidance on typical usage, not a live API or real-time count.

        Can hashtags get shadowbanned?

        Individual tags can occasionally get restricted if they're flooded with spam or policy-violating posts. If you're unsure, check the tag's own page on the platform before using it.

        Is this hashtag generator free?

        Yes — completely free, no account required, no limit on how many times you generate. Everything runs locally in your browser.
