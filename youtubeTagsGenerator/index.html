What Are YouTube Tags?

YouTube tags are hidden keywords entered in Studio's "Tags" field to describe a video's topic to YouTube's indexing system. Viewers never see them on the watch page — they're metadata, not on-screen text. They still exist and are still worth filling in, but YouTube itself has said their effect on discovery is modest compared to your title, thumbnail, and description.

The Real 500-Character Tag Limit

YouTube caps tags at 500 characters total — not per tag, but across every tag you add, including the commas that separate them in the underlying field. Once that combined string reaches 500 characters, Studio stops accepting more. This tool tracks that exact combined count live as you build your list.

Tag Best Practices

Lead with your single most important, most accurate tag — ideally your exact video topic — since it can double as YouTube's fallback video title in some placements. Mix broad category tags with specific long-tail phrases, and never add a tag that misleads viewers about what the video contains; YouTube can penalize videos for tag spam or clearly irrelevant tags.

How This Generator Builds Suggestions

It's a rule-based generator, not a connection to an AI model. It takes your topic, strips common stopwords (a, the, and, of...) to find the significant words, then combines the full phrase, each significant word, and your phrase against a set of proven modifiers like "tutorial", "guide", "for beginners", and "2026". If you pick a niche, it blends in a curated bank of generic popular tags for that category, then removes duplicates and caps the list around 30-35 tags.

Topic: "Beginner guitar lessons for adults", niche: Education. Frequently Asked Questions

What are YouTube tags?

Hidden keywords and phrases attached to a video in Studio's "Tags" field to describe its topic to YouTube's system. Viewers never see them on the page — they're metadata only.

Do tags help SEO/ranking or just discovery?

YouTube has said tags are a minor signal, mostly useful for catching common misspellings of your title's keywords and for topic context. Title, thumbnail, description, spoken content, and watch time matter far more for ranking.

What's the character limit?

500 characters total, combined across all your tags including the commas between them — not 500 characters per individual tag.

How many tags should I use?

There's no fixed number. Aim for accurate tags that reasonably fill the 500-character budget — typically 15-30 tags mixing your exact topic, single keywords, and a few longer phrases.

Tags vs titles vs description — what matters most for SEO?

Title and thumbnail drive click-through, your description's first lines and spoken/captioned content drive relevance matching, and audience retention drives ranking after that. Tags are a small supporting signal underneath all of it.

Is this tool free and private?

Yes — completely free, no account required, no limit on how many times you generate tags. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.
