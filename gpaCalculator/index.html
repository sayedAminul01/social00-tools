What is GPA and How is it Calculated?

GPA (Grade Point Average) is a standardized way of measuring academic achievement. It's calculated by converting letter grades to grade points, multiplying by credit hours, and dividing by total credits.

Formula: GPA = (Sum of Grade Points × Credit Hours) / Total Credit Hours

GPA to Percentage Conversion Formula

Converting GPA to percentage depends on your grading scale:

  • 4.0 Scale: Percentage = (GPA / 4.0) × 100
  • 5.0 Scale: Percentage = (GPA / 5.0) × 100
  • 10.0 Scale: Percentage = GPA × 9.5

Note: Different institutions may use different conversion methods. Always check with your school's policy.

Understanding CGPA vs GPA

GPA measures your performance in a single semester or term, while CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) represents your overall academic performance across all semesters.

CGPA is calculated by taking the weighted average of all semester GPAs, considering the credit hours of each semester.

How to Improve Your GPA

  • Attend all classes: Regular attendance improves understanding and grades
  • Complete assignments on time: Consistent work builds better grades
  • Study regularly: Daily study habits prevent last-minute cramming
  • Seek help early: Don't wait until you're struggling to ask for help
  • Focus on difficult subjects: Allocate more time to challenging courses
  • Use study groups: Collaborative learning enhances understanding
  • Take advantage of extra credit: Every point counts toward your GPA
