What Is Lorem Ipsum?

          
Lorem Ipsum is the default placeholder text of the design and publishing world — nonsense-Latin copy dropped into a layout, mockup or template so it looks like a finished page before any real writing exists. It has the rhythm and letter distribution of ordinary prose without being an actual, readable message.

        

        

          
The Real History: Cicero and 45 BC

          
The text traces back to a genuine classical source: a passage from Cicero's "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum" ("On the Ends of Good and Evil"), a Latin philosophical work written in 45 BC. Early typesetters scrambled its words and added Latin-looking filler, and the garbled result — not a real, translatable sentence — is what we now call Lorem Ipsum.

        

        

          
Why Designers Choose Nonsense Over Real Copy

          
Real, legible content is distracting when you're supposed to be judging a layout — the reader's eye and brain default to reading it rather than evaluating spacing, type size or hierarchy. Meaningless placeholder text sidesteps that entirely, letting typography and structure get judged on their own without any competing message.

        

        

          
Paragraphs, Sentences or Words — Picking a Unit

          
Use Paragraphs for mocking up article bodies or landing-page copy, Sentences for shorter blocks like captions or card descriptions, and Words for tight spaces such as buttons, labels or navigation items where you just need a handful of filler tokens.

        

        

          
Using the HTML Output for Web Design

          
Switch on HTML output while generating paragraphs and each paragraph is wrapped in <p>...</p> tags in the copied text, so it drops straight into a template or CMS field instead of needing manual markup afterward. It only affects Paragraphs mode — single sentences and words don't need paragraph tags.

Frequently Asked Questions

        

          

            What is Lorem Ipsum?
            
Lorem Ipsum is the standard placeholder (or "dummy") text used across design, publishing and web development to fill a layout with realistic-looking paragraphs before the real content is written. It's meaningless as language, but it looks and reads like ordinary prose in terms of word length and letter distribution.

          

          

            Where does Lorem Ipsum actually come from?
            
It's derived from a scrambled and altered passage of Cicero's "De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum," a classical Latin text on ethics written in 45 BC. Words were cut, reordered and Latin-sounding filler was added, which is why the result reads like Latin but isn't a real, translatable passage.

          

          

            Why do designers use meaningless text instead of real content?
            
Because real, readable content pulls a reviewer's attention into reading it rather than looking at the layout. Placeholder text has a normal, natural distribution of word and sentence lengths without being distracting or legible as an actual message, which keeps the focus on typography, spacing and structure instead.

          

          

            What does the "HTML output" toggle do?
            
When it's on and you're generating paragraphs, each paragraph is wrapped in <p>...</p> tags in the copied output, so you can paste it straight into HTML markup. It only applies in Paragraphs mode, since sentences and single words don't need paragraph tags.

          

          

            Is the text this tool generates real Latin, or made up?
            
Neither, exactly. Every word comes from the genuine, traditional Lorem Ipsum passage — this tool never invents new words — but the sentences themselves are freshly assembled by picking words from that passage at random, so no two generations are identical.

          

          

            Does this tool run locally, or send my data anywhere?
            
Everything runs locally in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing you generate, type or copy is ever sent to a server — there's no account, no tracking of your input, and no limit on how many times you generate.
