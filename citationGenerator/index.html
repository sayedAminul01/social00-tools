What is a Citation Generator?

A citation generator is an automated tool that creates properly formatted citations and references for academic papers, research projects, and scholarly work. It eliminates the tedious manual formatting process by automatically arranging author names, titles, dates, and other publication information according to specific citation style guidelines.

Our citation generator supports the three most widely used academic citation formats: APA (American Psychological Association), MLA (Modern Language Association), and Chicago style, ensuring your references meet academic standards.

APA Citation Format Guide

APA 7th Edition is commonly used in social sciences, psychology, education, and business. It uses an author-date citation system.

Basic Format:

  • Website: Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). Title of page. Website Name. URL
  • Book: Author, A. A. (Year). Title of book. Publisher.
  • Journal: Author, A. A. (Year). Title of article. Journal Name, Volume(Issue), pages. DOI

Example: Smith, J. D. (2023, March 15). Understanding citation formats. Academic Resources. https://example.com/citations

MLA Citation Format Guide

MLA 9th Edition is primarily used in humanities, literature, and language studies. It emphasizes the author and page number.

Basic Format:

  • Website: Author Last Name, First Name. "Title of Page." Website Name, Day Month Year, URL.
  • Book: Author Last Name, First Name. Title of Book. Publisher, Year.
  • Journal: Author Last Name, First Name. "Article Title." Journal Name, vol. #, no. #, Year, pp. #-#.

Example: Smith, John. "Understanding Citation Formats." Academic Resources, 15 Mar. 2023, example.com/citations.

Chicago Citation Format Guide

Chicago Manual of Style offers two systems: Notes-Bibliography (used in humanities) and Author-Date (used in sciences). Our generator uses the Author-Date system.

Basic Format:

  • Website: Author Last Name, First Name. Year. "Title of Page." Website Name. Accessed Month Day, Year. URL.
  • Book: Author Last Name, First Name. Year. Title of Book. Place: Publisher.
  • Journal: Author Last Name, First Name. Year. "Article Title." Journal Name Volume (Issue): pages.

Example: Smith, John. 2023. "Understanding Citation Formats." Academic Resources. Accessed March 15, 2023. https://example.com/citations.

Why Citations Matter

  • Academic Integrity: Citations give credit to original authors and prevent plagiarism
  • Credibility: Proper citations demonstrate thorough research and strengthen your arguments
  • Verification: Readers can locate and verify your sources independently
  • Scholarly Conversation: Citations connect your work to broader academic discussions
  • Legal Protection: Proper attribution protects you from copyright infringement
  • Professional Standards: Following citation guidelines shows attention to academic conventions

Common Citation Mistakes to Avoid

  • Missing information: Always include all required elements (author, date, title, source)
  • Incorrect punctuation: Pay attention to periods, commas, and italics placement
  • Wrong date format: Each style has specific date formatting requirements
  • Inconsistent formatting: Use the same citation style throughout your paper
  • Outdated style guides: Ensure you're using the latest edition (APA 7th, MLA 9th)
  • Missing access dates: Include access dates for online sources when required
  • Incorrect capitalization: Follow title case rules for each citation style
