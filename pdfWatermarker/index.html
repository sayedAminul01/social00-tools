What Is This Watermark Tool?

This tool stamps a custom text watermark — like "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", or your company name — onto every page of a PDF, or just the pages you choose. It uses PDF.js to render pages for the preview and pdf-lib to write the final watermarked file, both running entirely on your own device.

How the Watermark Is Centered

The watermark text is centered on the page after rotation is applied, using the text's real measured width and a rotation-aware offset — not a naive fixed anchor point. That means a 45° diagonal watermark lands with its visual center on the page's actual center, however wide the text or however large the page.

Customizing Your Watermark

Choose from the three PDF Standard 14 fonts (Helvetica, Times Roman, Courier), set the font size and color, and control how strongly it shows through with the opacity slider. The rotation slider lets you angle the text anywhere from -90° to 90°, with 45° as a classic diagonal default.

Watermarking Specific Pages

By default the watermark is applied to every page. Switch "Apply to" to Custom range and enter page numbers like 1-3,5 to mark only a subset — useful when a cover page or appendix shouldn't carry the same stamp as the body of the document.

Permanent, Not an Overlay

Unlike a sticky-note style annotation, the watermark this tool draws becomes part of the page's actual content — the same way the original text and images are part of it. That makes it far harder to accidentally strip out, but it also means there's no "remove watermark" button here: if you need a clean copy later, hold onto your original file.

Privacy: Everything Happens in Your Browser

  • Your PDF is read locally and never uploaded to a server
  • Rendering (PDF.js) and watermarking/export (pdf-lib) both run on your device
  • Nothing is stored, logged, or sent anywhere — close the tab and it's gone
  • Free, unlimited, no sign-up, no watermark on the watermark tool itself

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. This tool loads your PDF locally using PDF.js for rendering and pdf-lib for writing the watermark, both running entirely in your browser. Your file is never uploaded anywhere — the watermarked copy is generated on your own device.

Can I watermark a password-protected PDF?

Not currently. This tool cannot open PDFs that are encrypted or password-protected, since the underlying libraries (PDF.js and pdf-lib) require the file to be unlocked first. Remove the password with your PDF viewer, then upload the unlocked file.

Can I remove the watermark afterward?

No, and this is important to know before you export. The watermark is drawn as real, permanent page content — the same way the rest of the page's text is — not as a separate overlay or annotation layer that can be toggled off. If you need an unmarked copy later, keep your original file; there is no built-in way to strip the watermark back out.

Can I use a logo or image as the watermark instead of text?

Not in this version — only text watermarks are supported, since that covers the overwhelming majority of real use cases like "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", or a company name. Image/logo watermarking may be added in a future version.

Can I watermark only some pages instead of the whole document?

Yes. Choose "Custom range" and enter page numbers like 1-3,5 to watermark only those pages, leaving the rest of the document untouched. The default is to apply the watermark to every page.

Is this tool free?

Yes — completely free, no account required, no limit on file size or how many PDFs you watermark. Everything runs locally in your browser.
