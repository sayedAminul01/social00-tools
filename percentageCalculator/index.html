What is a Percentage Calculator?

A percentage calculator is a tool that computes percentage values instantly — saving you from mental math or formula recall. Whether you're figuring out a sale price, analyzing business growth, or checking a test score, percentages are everywhere. Our free online percentage calculator handles 7 types of calculations in one clean interface.

How to Calculate Percentages

The core percentage formula is: Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100. For example, to find what percent 30 is of 150: (30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%. To find X% of Y: (X ÷ 100) × Y. So 15% of 300 = (15 ÷ 100) × 300 = 45.

Percentage Increase & Decrease Explained

Percentage Increase formula: ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100. If a stock goes from $50 to $65, the increase is ((65−50)÷50) × 100 = 30%. Percentage Decrease uses the same formula — a negative result means it decreased. Great for tracking price drops, revenue changes, or weight loss.

Discount Calculation Guide

To find the final price after a discount: Final Price = Original Price × (1 − Discount% ÷ 100). A $120 item at 25% off: 120 × (1 − 0.25) = $90. Our discount calculator also shows you the exact savings amount, making it perfect for shopping comparisons.

Markup Calculator for Businesses

Markup is the percentage added to the cost price to arrive at a selling price: Selling Price = Cost × (1 + Markup% ÷ 100). If your product costs $80 and you apply a 25% markup, the selling price is 80 × 1.25 = $100. Use this to set profitable prices while staying competitive.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate what percent X is of Y?

Divide X by Y, then multiply by 100. Formula: (X ÷ Y) × 100. Example: 40 is what percent of 160? (40 ÷ 160) × 100 = 25%.

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Subtract the original from the new value, divide by the original, multiply by 100. Formula: ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100. If revenue grew from $10,000 to $13,000: ((13000−10000)÷10000) × 100 = 30%.

What is the formula for a discount?

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount% ÷ 100). Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount. Our calculator does both steps automatically.

What is the difference between markup and margin?

Markup is calculated on the cost price; Margin is calculated on the selling price. A 25% markup on $100 cost gives $125 selling price. That's a 20% margin (25÷125). Our markup calculator uses the cost-based formula.

How is grade percentage calculated?

Grade% = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100. If you scored 76 out of 100, your grade is 76%. Our calculator also shows the letter grade.
