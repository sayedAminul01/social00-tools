What is a URL Slug?

A URL slug is the human-readable part of a web address that identifies a specific page — everything after the domain and folder path. In social00.com/tools/url-slug-generator, the slug is url-slug-generator. A good slug is short, lowercase, hyphen-separated, and describes the page in plain keywords.

Why Slugs Matter for SEO

Search engines weigh keywords in the URL as a minor but real ranking signal, and readable slugs consistently earn higher click-through rates in search results than IDs, dates, or query strings. A clean slug also makes a link easier to trust, share, and remember.

How This Generator Works

Your text is lowercased, accented characters are normalised (é → e), common stopwords are optionally stripped, and everything that isn't a letter or number is collapsed into a single separator. The result is trimmed to your chosen max length at a word boundary — never cut off mid-word.

Slug Best Practices

Keep slugs under ~60 characters, use hyphens (not underscores — Google treats hyphens as word separators, not underscores), avoid stopwords like "a", "the", "and", and include your primary keyword near the start rather than the end.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a URL slug?

The readable identifier at the end of a web address, e.g. "url-slug-generator" in this page's own URL. It should describe the page in short, plain keywords.

Why do slugs matter for SEO?

Slug keywords are a minor Google ranking signal, and clean slugs get better click-through rates in search results than random IDs or long parameters.

Hyphens or underscores?

Hyphens. Google treats a hyphen as a word separator but reads an underscore as joining two words together, which hurts keyword matching.

Should I remove stopwords from a slug?

Generally yes — removing words like "a", "the", "of", "and" keeps the slug short and keyword-dense, which most SEO guidance recommends.

How long should a slug be?

Most guidance suggests keeping it under 60 characters so it doesn't get truncated in search results and stays easy to read and share.
